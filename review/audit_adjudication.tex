\section{Scope, material, and method}

Three independent line-level audits of the manuscript \emph{The Prime-Spectral
Framework: Theorems, Conjectures, and Numerical Tests} were received in
\texttt{audits/prime audit.txt}: \textbf{astra} (13 points, short-form),
\textbf{grok} (13 numbered flaws, a 7-row internal-inconsistency table, and a
7-item improvement program), and \textbf{muse} (8 subsections of mathematical
gaps, minor issues, and a 6-item improvement plan). This document adjudicates
them jointly: every substantive claim was checked against the manuscript text,
every checkable mathematical assertion was re-derived or computed, the
numerical demands that could be executed at laptop scale were executed, the
surviving fixes were folded into the manuscript, and the points on which the
audits disagree with each other were resolved on the mathematics.

\paragraph{Verdict categories.} Each point receives one of:
\ok{} (the claim is correct and a fix has been applied);
\pver{} (the concern is valid but the stated specifics are wrong or
overstated; a precise fix is applied instead);
\reb{} (the claim is factually wrong, with proof or computation);
\sat{} (the manuscript already contains the demanded content; the audit
misread or reviewed an extraction artifact);
\textbf{run} (the demand was executed as a new computation and folded in).

\paragraph{Verification tools.} (i) Exact and Monte-Carlo checks
(\texttt{scripts/adjudication\_checks.py}): counting-defect convergence,
conjugacy-class divisibility on 12 finite groups, the Dirichlet return-time
bound, and Erd\H{o}s--Kac normal-order simulation. (ii) New numerical controls
(\texttt{c4\_scaled\_eth.py --scramble}, \texttt{--sigma-quantile}):
label-scrambled diagonal controls, a generic-coupling quantile sweep, and
$1/\log D$ scaling fits. (iii) Line-level re-reading of all eight source
files of the manuscript.

\section{Verification computations}

\subsection{Counting-law defect (O1)}
At level $m$ the defect extremum is $-\log(1+1/m)$, and $1/m=e^{-x}$ at
$x=\log m$: amplitude ratio $\to1$ (computed to $m=10^{6}$ exactly and
$m=10^{100}$ by formula). The bound $-\log(1-e^{-x})\le 2e^{-x}$ holds at
$x=\log 2$ with slack ($0.693\le1.0$). \textbf{grok's non-convergence claim is
false; muse's estimate is sharp.}

\subsection{Conjugacy-class divisibility (O4)}
Brute force over $C_n$ ($n\le5$), $S_n$ ($n\le5$), $D_n$ ($n\le8$), $Q_8$:
every class size divides the group order, as orbit--stabilizer guarantees.
\textbf{grok's ``only in trivial cases'' is wrong}; the paper's $c_i\mid N$
with $N=|G|$ is exact.

\subsection{Dirichlet return-time bound (G9)}
For $m$ rationally independent frequencies, the pigeonhole grid with
$N=\lceil 2\pi/\delta\rceil$ produces $q\le N^m$ with all phases within
$2\pi/N$: verified for $m=2,3$ at $\delta=0.05$ and $m=4,5$ at coarser
$\delta$. This is the mechanism behind the new Proposition~(return bound)
$\tau_\varepsilon\le(2\pi C_F/\varepsilon)^{m_F}$, $C_F=2+K_Fm_F$.

\subsection{Arithmetic vs.\ generic recurrence scales (G9/O1)}
Simulated $\omega(n)$ near $x=10^{6},10^{12},10^{18}$: means $2.94,3.55,3.76$
vs.\ $\log\log x=2.63,3.32,3.72$. With $M$ integers near $x$,
$m_F\lesssim M\log\log x$, hence
$\tau\lesssim(C/\varepsilon)^{M\log\log x}=(S/k_B)^{O(1)}$: \emph{polynomial in
the entropy} for bounded support, against the generic-system scale
$\gtrsim\varepsilon^{-D}$ with $D=e^{S/k_B}$: \emph{double-exponential}. This
is precisely the recurrence-time separation grok demanded as the only
non-absorbable C2 falsifier.
