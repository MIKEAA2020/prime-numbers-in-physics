\section{The Consolidated Conjecture Register}\label{sec:register}

that lacks proof lives here, numbered, graded, and armed with a
falsifier. Grades: \textbf{A} (mathematically precise; partial evidence exists; actively
testable), \textbf{B} (well-formed; speculative; no current evidence beyond structural
fit), \textbf{C} (interpretive; tethered to A/B entries; not empirically separable at
present). The \emph{Depends on} column lists register entries whose failure collapses the
entry; the qualifier ``(int.)'' marks a dependency that is interpretive rather than logical ---
such an entry survives the parent's failure as an autonomous mathematical problem.

Three structural observations about the register. First, its \emph{physical depth ordering}
is
$\mathsf{C1}\Rightarrow\{\mathsf{C2},\mathsf{C3},\mathsf{C5},\mathsf{C6}\}\Rightarrow
\{\mathsf{C4},\mathsf{C7},\mathsf{C8}\}\Rightarrow\{\mathsf{C9},\mathsf{C10},
\mathsf{C11}\}\Rightarrow\mathsf{C12}$, with two cross-layer edges
($\mathsf{C11}\leftarrow\mathsf{C3}$: the hierarchy falsifier invokes $\mathsf{C3}$'s horizon
temperature; $\mathsf{C10}\leftarrow\mathsf{C4}$: the ratchet compounds along the cascade):
the zeta/black-hole identification is the root, and its confirmation or collapse propagates
deterministically through the physical plane. But the register is \emph{two-plane}: on the
mathematical plane, $\mathsf{C4}$, $\mathsf{C5}$, and the computational content of
$\mathsf{C9}$ (find a number field matching a branching table) are autonomous problems
that survive any verdict on $\mathsf{C1}$---which is precisely why they carry the register's
most economical tests. $\mathsf{C4}$'s listed dependency on $\mathsf{C1}$ is interpretive, not
logical: the arithmetic sector and $H_{\mathrm{tot}}^{(U,V)}$ exist unconditionally, so the
thermalization problem is well-posed even if the spectral-identification program collapses.
Second, four entries ($\mathsf{C1}$, $\mathsf{C3}$, $\mathsf{C4}$, $\mathsf{C5}$) have
\emph{purely mathematical} test routes: they can be advanced or refuted without any
experiment. Third, the register is conservative: no claim appears both as a theorem and as
a graded conjecture; where a strong reading is available but unproved, only the graded
conjecture is carried.

\section{Falsifiability, Predictions, and Test Programs}\label{sec:falsify}

\subsection{What the framework predicts that rivals do not}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
\item \emph{No proton decay, by construction.} The arithmetic GUT unifies through number
fields, not through a larger Lie group; baryon number is not gauged into a decay channel.
Absence of proton decay, a persistent embarrassment for $\mathrm{SU}(5)$-type models, is
here a structural feature. Conversely, observed proton decay would falsify
$\mathsf{C9}$ outright---a clean, dated test.
\item \emph{Branching statistics are group statistics.} Under $\mathsf{C9}$, unification-scale
branching data must organize into Chebotarev densities: rational numbers with denominators
dividing a Galois group order. This is a sharp, unusual, and cheap-to-check signature. The
check is now calibrated: for $\geq5$ channels at precision $\sigma\leq0.3\%$ with
$|G|\lesssim$ a few hundred, a common-group-order divisor fit has Monte-Carlo-calibrated
false-positive rate below $1\%$ (the divisor constraint is what saves the test; the weak
form---each ratio near some rational---is vacuous because $\Qq$ is dense). Current tables
(e.g.\ the $Z$-pole branching fractions) admit no such fit at either granularity: the
five-channel scan and a seven-channel scan against a frozen 2024 PDG extract
(pre-registered protocol, null-first) both reject every common-$N$ table---the signature is
absent below unification, exactly as $\mathsf{C9}$ requires, and no obtainable data set can
decide the conjecture today. The computation validates and calibrates the instrument; the
purchase waits on unification-scale data.
\item \emph{A single new scale.} The framework has exactly one dimensionful parameter,
$\omega_{0}$, with $T_{H}=\hbar\omega_{0}/k_{B}$. Every other number in the physics layer
is either an integer, a group order, or a value of an $L$-function. Any phenomenological
use of the framework that requires a second free scale has already left the theory.
\item \emph{Entropy log-corrections are sawtooth-bounded, not logarithmic.} The
$O(1)$ defect in Theorem~\ref{thm:logcount} oscillates and never converges; the
framework's black-hole entropy corrections are therefore not of the standard
$-\alpha\log A$ family unless additional coarse-graining is applied. This is a distinctive
statistical signature of integer-lattice counting (register $\mathsf{C3}$'s falsifier).
\item \emph{Recurrence phenomenology is layered and cutoff-controlled.} The framework's
cosmology distinguishes exact recurrence (forbidden for generic states,
Theorem~\ref{thm:noperiod}) from $\varepsilon$-recurrence (universal for the free flow,
Theorem~\ref{cor:nopoincare}, but with return times growing without bound in the state),
and identifies genuine transience only in the cascading dynamics with three or more active
modes (Proposition~\ref{prop:polya}). Any completed theory of the arithmetic cutoff must
fix where our universe sits among these layers---a prediction in the sense that the cutoff
mechanism cannot be indifferent.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Falsifier taxonomy: mathematical and physical classes}

The register's falsifiers divide by their \emph{mode of decision} into two classes.
\emph{Mathematical} falsifiers are decidable by proof or by exact finite computation---no
physical input is required, and the decision, once obtained, is permanent. \emph{Physical}
falsifiers divide further into \emph{simulation-bounded} routes, whose reach is set by
measured compute ceilings (fill memory, Krylov horizons) and which deliver evidence rather
than verdicts, and \emph{observation-bounded} routes, which wait on data. The split matters
operationally: the mathematical class carries the register's cheapest decisive tests, the
simulation class is where the two executed programs live, and the observation class carries
the only dated falsifiers.

\paragraph{Mathematical falsifiers (proof or exact computation).}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
\item \emph{Thermalization of $H_{\mathrm{tot}}^{(U,V)}$} ($\mathsf{C4}$): a proof that the
diagonal ensemble converges to microcanonical along box-truncation sequences at fixed dose
would confirm; a proof of an obstruction (a conserved quantity blocking relaxation beyond
$\Nhat_{\mathrm{tot}}$, or a bound keeping $|\mathrm{diag}-\mathrm{micro}|$ bounded away from
zero) would refute. Proposition~\ref{prop:infinitevolume} supplies the well-posedness
premise: the infinite-volume operator exists, is self-adjoint and bounded below, and the
truncations converge in the strong resolvent sense, so both outcomes are meaningful
statements about one object. The $M$-sector decomposition used there (finite Jacobi blocks
per hopping pair) is also the natural starting structure for either proof.
\item \emph{Level-statistics class and the Hilbert--P\'olya construction} ($\mathsf{C5}$):
whether the infinite lattice model's statistics are GOE, GUE, or neither is a
computation-or-proof question, as is the existence of a fluctuation-sector operator with
spectrum $\{\tfrac12+i\gamma\}$; a proven non-GUE spectrum for the singularity sector would
falsify $\mathsf{C5}$ outright.
\item \emph{Chebotarev dictionary closure} ($\mathsf{C9}$): for any fixed branching table,
the search for a number field whose conjugacy-class proportions reproduce it is finite and
exact; one completed instance converts the dictionary from analogy to theory, and a proof
that no field realizes a table that the framework requires would break the dictionary.
\item \emph{Entropy oscillation structure} ($\mathsf{C3}$): the $O(1)$ sawtooth of
Theorem~\ref{thm:logcount} is exactly computable; its incompatibility with any specific
computed correction family (e.g.\ $-\tfrac32\log A$ terms) is a decidable comparison.
\item \emph{Recurrence Diophantine structure} ($\mathsf{C2}$): the return-time asymptotics
and their cutoff dependence are number-theoretic and exactly computable for any postulated
cutoff.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Physical falsifiers (simulation or observation).}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
\item \emph{ETH scaling diagnostics} ($\mathsf{C4}$, simulation): the accessible regime is
quantitatively bounded, and the bounds are themselves measured---interior eigen-windows by
the $LU$ fill ceiling ($D\leq20736$ on 4D grids at $3$--$4$\,GB; beyond it, folded-operator
restarted Lanczos at three subspace budgets, ILU$^{2}$-preconditioned LOBPCG, and
degree-$\sim\!5\times10^{3}$ polynomial filters were all measured to fail or stall on the
window-edge continuum), Krylov equilibration by the trajectory horizon ($\tau\approx40$--$50$
per day-scale compute at $D\approx10^{5}$). Within this regime the evidence is uniform: flat
$\sigma_{\mathrm{ETH}}/\mathrm{std}$ at the median window to $D=5.9\times10^{4}$, flat at the
upper-edge Krylov window to $D=1.05\times10^{5}$, no closing of the diagonal-to-microcanonical
gap, GOE statistics at generic coupling throughout.
\item \emph{Branching statistics as group statistics} ($\mathsf{C9}$, observation): the
instrument is calibrated and executed (five channels on the earlier table; seven channels on
the frozen 2024 extract, pre-registered protocol); unification-scale data remain the missing
input.
\item \emph{Observed proton decay} ($\mathsf{C9}$, observation): the cleanest dated
falsifier---absence is a structural feature, observation is terminal for $\mathsf{C9}$.
\item \emph{Initial-state smoothness and two-sided statistics} ($\mathsf{C7}$, $\mathsf{C8}$,
observation): CMB constraints on one- versus two-sided low-entropy origins.
\item \emph{Near-singularity equilibration} ($\mathsf{C6}$, simulation): BKL-map and
near-singularity GR computations exhibiting (or excluding) critical behavior at the Hagedorn
scale.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Assessment}

The framework now satisfies three standards. Its mathematical core is genuinely theorem-grade: the
spectrum, the
partition function, the Hagedorn asymptotics, the counting law, the clock kernel, the
no-periodicity theorem, and the entanglement structure are proved. Its analogies are
tightened: every structural analogy is either
a citable theorem (Kronecker--Weber, Chebotarev, Erd\H{o}s--Kac, P\'olya,
Bohr) or an explicit register entry. And its remaining ambition---that the primes order the
spectrum of spacetime itself---is stated in a form precise enough to be wrong:
$\mathsf{C1}$ through $\mathsf{C12}$ name their evidence, their dependencies, and their
falsifiers, and the two numerically testable entries ($\mathsf{C4}$, $\mathsf{C9}$) carry
carried-out programs with stated uncertainties. A framework that can be wrong in stated
ways is a research program; one that
cannot is a metaphor.
